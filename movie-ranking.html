The Best Movies according to Ebruce

My top 5 movies of all time

Interstellar

Great movie with amazing storytelling and heartwarming moments

Godzilla x Kong: The New Dawn

Amazing story that links to the other 2 movies with awesome fight scenes plus some funny and unexpected moments

Ready Player One

I've never read the book but i loved everything about the movie from start to finish and will read the book

The Incredibles 1 & 2

Hope to see a 3rd one to finish it off as both were spectacular, had great fight scenes, and lovely effects

Spiderman: Across the Spiderverse + Beyond the Spider-Verse

One of the greatest animated movies i've ever seen in my life with amazing storytelling, the second movie took it up a notch and was even better than the first one! I can't wait for the third movie of the trilogy and how it all ends
